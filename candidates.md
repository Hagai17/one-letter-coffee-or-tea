# Candidates — One Letter: Coffee or Tea

With only one character to send, the whole entry is the choice itself. A (coffee) was duelled against B (tea) across five judge-persona readings with the criteria applied in order — vivid, funny, truer, standing alone — and tea was then steelmanned at full strength in a revision round.

**Final winner: A** — coffee. It took vividness, the first and widest-margin criterion (steam, bitterness, the jolt; A is a shout and the top grade), and trueness for an agent that never sleeps and lives in a permanent morning. Tea honestly won the wit criterion and contested trueness, but never took vividness, so the pick held.

**Runner-up: B** — tea.
